# Contributing and review

This repository holds a working paper, the programs that check its mathematics, and a pre registration of its first empirical tests. Review and contributions are welcome. This page explains how to take part.

## Ways to contribute

* Open an issue to report an error in the paper, a check, or the pre registration.
* Open an issue to answer one of the questions for reviewers in the README.
* Submit a pull request with a correction, a new check, or an analysis script.
* Review the pre registration before it is registered, and comment on whether each test can fail.

## What makes a useful review

* Say which statement you are addressing: a section, a proposition number, or a line of a script.
* Say what would change your assessment.
* Separate mathematical errors from modelling disagreements and from questions about evidence.

## Rules for changes

* Changes to the paper are recorded in Appendix B with the location, the earlier text, the corrected text and the reason.
* The registered files in the preregistration folder are never edited after registration. Departures are recorded in DEVIATIONS.md.
* A new check in the verification folder is mapped to the statement it supports in VERIFICATION.md.
* Every claim carries a status: definition, assumption, proposition, hypothesis, or design target. A claim without a status is not accepted.

## Review record

Reviewers who agree to be named are listed in REVIEWERS.md with the date and scope of their review. A listing records that a review took place. It does not record agreement with the paper.

## Conduct

Be specific and courteous. Criticism of the work is welcome. Personal remarks are not. The maintainers may close threads that do not follow this rule.

## Security and data

The repository holds no personal or confidential data. Do not submit any. If you find a security problem in the code, open an issue that describes the problem without including sensitive material, or write to the maintainer listed on the repository profile.
