# Pre registration

This folder holds the pre registration of the first empirical tests of the friction entropy index.

## Files

* PREREGISTRATION_v1_1.md and PREREGISTRATION_v1_1.pdf: the current version. It supersedes version 1.0.
* archive/PREREGISTRATION_v1_0.md: version 1.0, kept unchanged as a record. It was committed on 31 October 2026 and amended before any data access.
* AMENDMENT_A1.md and AMENDMENT_A1.pdf: Amendment A1, 12 November 2026, design decisions for S1 made before any data were loaded.
* AMENDMENT_A1_1.md and AMENDMENT_A1_1.pdf: Amendment A1.1, 12 November 2026, two clarifications to A1 after reviewer comments. Where A1.1 speaks, it wins over A1.
* AMENDMENT_A2.md and AMENDMENT_A2.pdf: Amendment A2, 14 November 2026, the data freeze: the 28 Ethereum files with checksums and the D1 status. The manifests are in manifests/.
* REGISTRY.md: every document with its date, SHA256, GitHub release and DOI.
* DEVIATIONS.md: the log of departures from the registered plan and of amendments, each with its date and whether it was made before data access.

## Registration

The pre registration (version 1.1) was registered publicly on OSF Registries on 1 November 2026.

* Registration: https://osf.io/w6uan/
* DOI: https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/W6UAN
* Archive: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23869943

The registered files are not edited after registration. Any change is made by a dated amendment and recorded in DEVIATIONS.md. Amendments A1 and A1.1 are registered on OSF at https://osf.io/ezpxf, and A2 at https://osf.io/pt43h.

## Data freeze amendment

Before any validation or test data are loaded, a dated amendment records exact file versions, row counts, SHA256 checksums, download dates, the chosen window length and bin edges (from the training period only), and the chain data source. It is committed and registered before the validation and test periods are opened. See Section 5 of the registration.

## Reporting

The results of every registered test are published in this repository within 30 days of completing the analysis, whatever the outcome.
